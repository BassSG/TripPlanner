# Assets

- `assets/chiang-mai.jpg`: Ash Edmonds, sunset outside Chiang Mai. [Original on Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/landscape-photo-of-mountains-mXBZo6PvjMk), [Unsplash license](https://unsplash.com/license). Downloaded as a 1400px JPEG for reliable, local hosting and offline use.
- `assets/brand-mark.svg`: original TripPlanner route-arrow artwork in muted sky blue and champagne gold, on navy ink.
- `assets/icon.svg`, `icon-16.png` through `icon-1024.png`, `favicon.ico`: all generated from the same mark by `scripts/icons.ps1`; 180px is the Apple touch icon, 192/512px are the PWA icons. Essential shapes fit the maskable safe area.
- `assets/logo-wordmark-dark.svg` / `logo-wordmark-light.svg` and their transparent PNGs: horizontal brand lockups with outlined letterforms, so the SVGs do not require an installed font.
- `assets/logo-monochrome.svg`: single-color transparent mark for restrained use. [Brand guide](BRAND.md) documents colors and regeneration.
- Interface icons: original inline SVG paths in `src/app.js`.
- Fonts: Manrope and Noto Sans Thai, served by Google Fonts, with local system fallbacks when offline.
